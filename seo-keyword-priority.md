## 3. Search intent by page type

### Commercial landing pages
Intent: ready to buy or enquire
Goal: provide product clarity, pricing context, location context, and clear CTA

### Informational pages
Intent: learning / comparison / research
Goal: build trust, authority, and internal links

### Conversion pages
Intent: direct enquiry
Goal: lead form, WhatsApp, call, brochure request

## 4. Messaging priorities

Use these content themes:
- premium apartments in Riverside, Nairobi
- 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments
- Riverside address and location
- investment and lifestyle appeal
- safe, high-quality residential environment
- community and amenities

## 5. Rules for keyword use

- Do not stuff the exact phrase repeatedly into one paragraph.
- Use the key phrase naturally in the title, H1, intro, and one or two supporting sections.
- Build semantic variety around the core phrase: Riverside Azure, Riverside Nairobi apartments, Riverside Drive apartments, apartment investment Nairobi.
- Use descriptive anchor text for internal links.
